# Nomi 贡献政策（不要求 CLA）

Nomi 现在只发布 **[AGPL-3.0-only](LICENSE)** 版本，不要求贡献者签署 Contributor License Agreement（CLA），也没有机器人签字口令。提交 Pull Request、报告问题或参与讨论，都不需要额外签字。

贡献者保留自己贡献内容的版权；提交贡献即表示你有权按 AGPL-3.0-only 将它提供给本项目。项目会和其他 AGPL 代码一样，保留必要的版权、许可证和来源说明。

这个文件保留在仓库中，只是为了让旧链接不会误导贡献者。它不是合同，不需要回复、勾选或签署。Nomi 不提供只交安装包而不提供相应源代码的闭源版本；我们可以收费提供遵守 AGPL 的定制开发、集成、部署、培训和持续迭代服务。

## English

Nomi ships only under **[AGPL-3.0-only](LICENSE)**. Contributors do not need to sign a Contributor License Agreement (CLA), and there is no bot phrase or signature ledger. Opening a pull request, reporting an issue, or joining a discussion requires no extra signature.

Contributors keep the copyright in their work and submit it under AGPL-3.0-only, provided they have the right to do so. This file remains only to keep old links from misleading contributors; it is informational, not a contract, and requires no reply or checkbox.

Nomi does not offer a closed-source build that withholds the corresponding source code. Paid services may include AGPL-compliant custom development, integration, deployment, training, and ongoing iteration.
