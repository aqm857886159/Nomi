Source: https://chatcut.io/docs/editor-overview (accessed 2026-09-05)
- Default view puts AI and media controls beside Viewer, Timeline below.
- Top bar includes Undo, Redo, Workspace, Versions, Export.
- Viewer displays current playhead frame.
- Playback controls above Timeline include split, snapping, play/pause, time, zoom, canvas aspect ratio, captions, fullscreen.
- Timeline has video tracks above audio tracks; track header controls visibility and audio.

Source: https://chatcut.io/docs/timeline (accessed 2026-09-05)
- Timeline runs left to right; yellow playhead marks current Viewer frame.
- Zoom controls change visible time; right-click exposes clip operations and empty-area Zoom to fit.
