1. How to edit video in CapCut using essential editing tools
Getting to know the basic editing tools on CapCut PC is the key to transforming your content. They are the basis and foundation of your video and audio. These essential features include:
Split:
Divide a video clip into multiple segments at a chosen point.
Crop:
Change the dimensions of a video or image to fit a desired frame size.
Freeze:
Create a still frame from a specific moment in your video.
Reverse:
Play a video clip backward for a unique effect.
Rotate:
Tilt or turn a video or image by degrees for different orientations.
Flip:
Mirror a video or image horizontally or vertically.
How to make an edit on CapCut using its basic editing tools
STEP
1
Import
Click "New project" once CapCut opens to access the editing page. You can now drag a video or audio from a folder and drop it on the timeline. Alternatively, you can click "Import" and use File Explorer to locate your media and add it to the editor. You can also use files from your CapCut space or leverage stock videos from CapCut's library.
2
Edit
Click the video or audio file on your CapCut timeline to access the essential editing tools. They are located just above the timeline track. From the left side, you have the "Split" tool to create dynamic cuts or remove unwanted sections. The next tool is "Delete left" which instantly removes the left side section after splitting. "Delete right" does the opposite. Click "Delete" to remove an entire track from the timeline.
The other tool is "Add marker" to indicate important points in the videos. You can also freeze a moment or reverse a video to add an element of intrigue and surprise. Other features are a mirror to flip a video, rotate to change footage orientation, and resize to crop vital parts. These are helpful when remixing, reshuffling, and repurposing content.
3
Export
After editing your video to your standards, click "Export". This allows you to edit the video cover, add a title, and select a destination to save the edited output. You also have options to change the frame rate up to 60fps, resolution up to 4K, format (MOV/MP4), codec, and bit rate. If you are dealing with audio files, check the box next to "Export audio". It lets you change the audio format to MP3, WAV, or FLAC. Running a copyright check for content meant for online consumption is also recommended. Turn on the "Run a copyright check" toggle button and click "Export".
In the next export window, you will see the quick sharing tabs for TikTok and YouTube. If your edited content was meant for TikTok, set the aspect ratio to 9:16. CapCut also allows you to add a title, set visibility, and allow commenting, duets, and stitches. For YouTube videos, ensure you use the correct aspect ratio of 16:9. Click "Share" to instantly post your video on either social media platform. You don't need to exit CapCut.
2. How to make edits on CapCut using advanced video editing features
CapCut offers a surprising suite of advanced tools for content creators seeking more granular control. These empower you to create truly professional-looking edits to make your videos stand out. They include:
Keyframes:
Allow you to set precise points of change for video and audio properties.
Masks:
Enable you to isolate specific areas of your video or image for selective editing.
Transform:
Gives you control over the scaling, rotation, and positioning of video clips and image layers.
Blend:
Provides different blending modes to create visually interesting interactions between multiple video and image layers.
Adjustment:
Offers a range of tools for fine-tuning image and video properties such as brightness, contrast, and color balance.
Speed curve:
Let you precisely control the speed of your video over time
How to make CapCut video using advanced editing tools
Add your video to the CapCut video editor. Click on the timeline video to access CapCut's editing tools. Most advanced features are located on the right panel.
To take precision to the next level, add keyframes. Click on the point of the video to add an element, and then click the keyframe icon next to the effect you intend to apply. Set specific adjustments at designated points in your timeline. For example, you can zoom in on a specific detail in a photo or smoothly animate the position of text throughout a video.
Another advanced feature is masking. Click "Video" in the right panel and select "Mask". This unlocks creative possibilities, letting you isolate elements and apply selective edits. The transform tool adds a playful or eye-catching touch, whereas blend modes offer unique visual overlays.
Click "Adjustment" in the right panel to access color correction settings. Click "Basic" for essential color settings, and "HSL" for hue, saturation, and brightness. Click "Curves" to access the color curves and take control over color correction at specific points of video and other visual properties.
Click "Speed" > "Curve" to access the various speed curve options to create nuanced slow-motion buildups or dynamic accelerations. You'll see preset speed curves like a bullet, montage, jump cut, hero time, flash in, and flash out. You can also customize the speed curve to your needs.
The possibilities of CapCut's advanced feature set are truly endless!
Once all your edits are intact, click "Export". The CapCut desktop video editor has a download option and quick sharing to YouTube and TikTok.

4. How to use CapCut step by step and leverage its pro features
Although most features are free of charge, the CapCut desktop video editor offers some pro tools at pocket-friendly rates. You can experience these paid features for free but won't be able to export the output without a subscription. These include features like:
Vocal isolation:
Separate vocals from background music in your audio tracks.
Camera tracking:
Analyze and follow the movement of objects or people within your videos.
Auto reframe:
Automatically adapt your video's composition to fit different social media aspect ratios.
AI movement:
Simulate camera movements like panning, zooming, or shaking for dynamic effects.
How to make videos on CapCut using pro editing tools
Add your video to the CapCut PC editing window and click on it to access pro editing tools.
Click "Video" on the right panel and open the "Basic" tab. Click the checkbox next to "Removing video flickers" to eliminate those distracting inconsistencies for a clean video.
Click "Auto reframe" to reframe the video to your preferred aspect ratio. This function also lets you set image stabilization and camera moving speed.
To use camera tracking, click on your timeline video, copy it, and paste it above the original track. Go to the right menu and click "Video". Click "Motion tracking", and set the direction and turn on scale and distance.
For audio, click the "Audio" tab on the right panel. Under "Basic", scroll and click the checkbox next to "Vocal isolation". Use the drop-down arrow to select whether to remove or keep vocals. This allows you to separate the voice track and instrumental track to use each separately.
Click the checkbox next to "Enhance voice" to eliminate popping sounds, echoes, and mouth clicks for increased audio clarity. You can also use the slider and drag it to the left or right to control the enhanced voice effect's intensity.
After the editing process, click "Export" and save the video or instantly share it on YouTube and TikTok.

How do you use CapCut to add voiceovers to videos?
CapCut makes adding voiceovers incredibly easy. First, position the playhead on your video timeline where you want the voiceover to begin. Click the "Record" icon to open CapCut's built-in recorder and speak clearly while watching the video playback. When you're finished, click the record button again to stop it. This will create a new timeline track for the recorded voice. You can then adjust the voiceover's position to sync perfectly with the video and control the volume as needed. Suppose you want your voice to sound anonymous; use voice-changing filters like echo, deep, tremble, synth, etc.

How to make a CapCut edit without losing video quality?
To minimize quality loss when editing with CapCut PC, focus on your source video, editing choices, and export settings. Ensure you start with high-quality footage and use editing features like cropping or color adjustments sparingly. This helps to avoid multiple rounds of compression. Finally, during video exportation, you should prioritize the highest resolution and frame rate. CapCut supports up to 4K resolution and a 60 fps frame rate.
