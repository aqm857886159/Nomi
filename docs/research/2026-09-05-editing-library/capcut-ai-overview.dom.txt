If you're looking to create videos quickly and effortlessly, CapCut has got you covered. Imagine transforming your ideas into polished videos in no time! With CapCut’s AI writer and script-to-video tool, you can generate engaging scripts and full videos just by inputting your prompts. Want to add a professional touch? Use the AI text-to-voice tool to type your text and have it seamlessly read out in your video, perfect for narrations or voiceovers.

Let’s take a deep dive into all the great AI features you can use in CapCut.
CapCut AI media resources
AI stock
If you want to get creative and have no media to start with, you can use any of CapCut’s AI-based stock videos and clips. Customize it the way you want to create your video. You also have access to unlimited royalty-free stock footage you can use to complement your existing video content.
AI stickers
Liven up your videos with dynamic stickers generated by AI. Select one based on the emotion or theme of your video (e.g., "celebration," "birthday"). CapCut has so many options for you to choose from and you can even animate stickers that match your concept.
AI text
You have access to unlimited customized text presets that you can add to your videos. You can choose from different categories, such as social media, WordArt, Halloween, Valentine's, etc. You can also type in your own text that you want to include in your video and customize it.
CapCut AI tools for social media
Long video to shorts
As a content creator, you may have a long video that you want to turn into a captivating short video for social media. You can transform your
long video into shorts
with CapCut. You can identify the key moments, like changes in the scene, key appearances, or interesting visuals, and create shorter clips that capture the essence of the original video.
Auto reframe
Let's say you have a video shot vertically for your phone, but you now want to upload it to YouTube in a horizontal format. CapCut's
video resizer
intelligently analyzes the content of your video and automatically adjusts the frame to fit the new aspect ratio you want. It prioritizes keeping the most important elements (people, objects) in the frame while adjusting the size.
AI movement
AI movement is a new feature in CapCut that uses AI to smooth out shaky footage. This is especially handy if you want to stabilize a shaky handheld video clip. It analyzes the motion in your video and compensates for camera movements, creating a more polished and professional look.
Remove filler words
If you have a long lecture video where the speaker uses a lot of filler words like “um,” “uh,” and “you know,” you can automatically remove them using CapCut’s Remove Filler Words tool. This feature uses speech recognition to identify filler words in your video. You can choose to automatically remove them entirely or replace them.
CapCut AI tools for marketing
AI writer
CapCut’s AI Writer is a great AI-based tool for writing scripts for your video. You probably have an idea for your videos, campaign, or creative project but don’t have a written script to go by and create your video. You just need to input your prompt or description into the tool, and it will generate the perfect script for you.
Script to video
Imagine you have a script for a product explainer video but lack the footage. Simply feed your script into CapCut's
script-to-video maker
, and the AI video generator will generate video clips, images, and even background music that matches the content for you. You can then edit and customize it for a complete video.
AI character
Let’s say you want to create an engaging educational video but don’t have time for animation. This AI feature allows you to create a digital character that can lip-sync to a pre-recorded script or your own voice. After using the text-to-speech tool, you can choose from various character designs and customize the appearance.
Text-to-speech
Now, you can convert your text into a voiceover video with CapCut. You can select from various narrator styles and tones. All you have to do is type your desired text, and it will be read out loud in a video. You can go ahead and customize the video to give it a professional look.
More CapCut AI features that can enhance your video viewership
CapCut uses AI to analyze the audio in your video and transcribe the spoken words. It then converts the transcription into captions displayed on your video, making it accessible to a wider audience.
Auto adjust
While editing your videos, you can automatically adjust them for your specific needs. CapCut analyzes the footage and automatically adjusts lighting, color, and contrast across all clips, creating a more visually cohesive final video.
Enhance image quality
Let's say you have some low-resolution footage that appears blurry or pixelated. You can use CapCut to upscale video resolution and reduce image noise to create a clearer and more polished look.
This CapCut AI feature helps you isolate and reduce unwanted background noise, keeping the focus on the music and vocals. So now you can create a cleaner and more professional-sounding audio track.
Remove flickers
Perhaps you used uneven artificial lighting while filming indoors, causing flickering effects in your video. Easily detect and remove flickering frames, resulting in a smoother, more stable video playback experience.
AI video translator
If you need to transcribe your video into another language, CapCut's AI
video translator
analyzes your video, generates captions, and translates them into your chosen language. This could overlay subtitles or even replace signs and spoken words with the translated text.

The versatile toolkit comes with comprehensive editing features and advanced editing options like Keyframe Animations, Auto Captions, Vocal Isolation, etc., all presented in a clear and organized interface. Let’s take a look at how to use AI on CapCut. First, click on the link below to download the CapCut desktop video editor and follow along.
STEP
1
Import media
Click “Import” after launching the CapCut desktop PC editor and select your media to work on. You can upload multiple files at a time. You can also drag and drop your media onto the timeline to get started. Alternatively, you can also go to "Spaces" and import one from your CapCut space, or utilize a
free stock video
from CapCut's "Stock materials".
2
Edit video with AI tools
Now, customize your video with the available features for free. Click on the video in the timeline and enhance it using the editing features in the right pane. You can remove video shakiness and flickers, adjust the video playback speed, add animations, reduce background noise, normalize loudness, remove vocals, etc, to complete the video. Or you can utilize CapCut's rich media resources such as effects, filters, AI characters, etc to polish your video.
3
Export and share
After your edits, click "Export" from the top right corner to download your video. Adjust the video's title, customize the resolution (up to 4K), bit rate, codec, format (MP4 or MOV), and frame rate (up to 60fps), and click on "Export" to get your video downloaded. You can share your video with TikTok and YouTube directly from the app.

Is CapCut AI safe?
CapCut’s AI features are completely safe to use. It is used by a lot of users, and there have been no reports of it being malicious or harmful to devices. You can use CapCut’s AI tools to edit and customize your videos without worrying about the safety of your media content.
Can CapCut do AI voice?
Yes, you can add AI voices to your videos in CapCut. You can use the text-to-speech tool to convert your written text into narration using AI. There are a variety of AI voices to choose from. You just need to select a voiceover for your videos without recording your voice or hiring a voice actor.
How to use AI characters in CapCut?
You need to use the text option to add “Default text.” Edit and customize the text you want to be narrated, then use the text-to-speech tool to set the tone and apply it. Next, use the AI character option and select the character you want to display while reading the text. You can further customize the background and edit the full video.
