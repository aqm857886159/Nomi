How to add sound effects to a video
Step 1: Upload video
Log in to CapCut online and click "Create new" from the homepage.Once on the canvas, click "Upload" under "Media" section,Upload media files from the computer,
Google Drive
, Dropbox, Myspace, or by QR code.
Step 2: Add sound effects to video
In the left sidebar of the CapCut online editor, click on the "Audio" tab.Within the "Audio" section, you will see a sub-section for "Sound effects". Click on it to browse the library.You can browse through categories (e.g., Magic, Laugh, Transition, Fight, Horror, etc.).Alternatively, use the search bar to find a specific effect (e.g., "whoosh," "click," "explosion").Once you find a sound effect you like, click the "+" button next to it, or drag and drop the audio clip directly onto your video timeline.Feel free to edit the audio clip as required via custom settings with the audio editor.
Step 3: Export and share
Once you are satisfied, click the "Export" button in the top-right corner of the editor.Set the file name, resolution (e.g., 1080p, 4K), frame rate, and format (e.g., MP4).Click "Export" again. CapCut will process your video.Alternatively, you can
share it directly
on social media platforms like TikTok or YouTube.
