# Nomi v0.24.0 — 界面换新，画布上能直接剪视频、改图、看历史版本

这一版改动多。打开就能看到界面换了：顶栏合成一条，左边多了一条窄栏，Agent 可以缩成小球、浮成小窗或停靠在右侧。常用入口有挪位置的，文末「升级须知」逐条写了以前在哪、现在在哪。画布上的节点能直接剪视频、截帧、改图、翻看历史版本；大项目打开和拖动更快；跟花钱有关的确认、取消和取回，说得更准。

**从 0.23 升级**：0.23 及更早的版本不会自己提示这次更新。请在 Nomi 里打开「设置 › 关于」，点「检查更新」；或者到官网下载安装包。从 0.24 起，Nomi 会自己检查更新。

## 画布节点能做的更多

![出过几版的节点，点角标后各版本就地铺开](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aqm857886159/Nomi/v0.24.0/docs/release-notes/v0.24.0/zh-CN/node-versions.png)

- **就地看历史版本**：节点出过 2 版以上，图片右上角有一个数字角标。点它，各版本在节点旁铺成宫格；鼠标移到某一版上，可以「设为主图」、下载或删除。设为主图可以按 Ctrl/⌘+Z 撤回。（#1054、#1050）
- **视频直接剪**：在视频节点浮条上点「剪辑」，拖两端选出要留的一段，再点「剪辑片段」，旁边会出一张连好线的新视频卡。剪辑在你电脑上完成，不调用生成模型。（#1156、#1176）
- **视频截帧**：「截帧 ▾」里取首帧或尾帧；视频卡底栏的相机钮取正在播放的这一帧。截出来是一张连好线的图片卡。（#1152）
- **画板就地打开**：画板不再弹出全屏窗口，在画布上原位编辑，打开就是画笔；画完的内容直接显示在节点上。（#1189、#1292、#1352）
- **文字节点**：选中后下面是「文本加工」框，可以扩写成提示词、看图写描述、翻译、拆成多条、续写或改写。把它连到图片或视频节点，生成时这段字接在提示词后面。Agent 也能读、能改它的正文。（#1137）
- **一键派生，不自动开跑**：图片浮条上的「多机位九宫格」，以及 ▾ 里的下一刻、前一刻、三视图、剧情四宫格，点一下就在旁边建好一个连着原图的节点、填好提示词，你点 ↑ 才生成。（#1001）
- **撤销只撤你自己的编辑**：按 Ctrl/⌘+Z 不会再把已经出好的图、写好的文字一起撤掉；生成进行中按撤销，节点也不会被拉回「空闲」。（#1065、#1073）
- **编组框好用了**：选中编组框，上方出现组工具条，有「颜色」「排列」「生成整组」「进时间轴」；拖动组框，里面的卡跟着走。（#1048、#1174、#1170）
- **拉环只出现在用得上的一侧**：选中节点后点左右的「+」圈，左边是「给它加输入」，右边是「用这个节点生成」，不用拖线。（#1133）

## Agent 与花钱

- **权限只剩两档**：「请求批准」是默认档：能撤销的事直接做，要生成先停下来等你确认。「直接生成」：要生成就直接开跑；同一镜连续失败，或要做计划外的生成时，会停下来问你。从「请求批准」切到「直接生成」要先确认一次。（#1203）
- **确认卡指向画布上的节点**：卡上是这几镜的小卡和一行摘要（模型、比例、清晰度）。想改哪一镜，点小卡跳到画布上那个节点去改；点「生成 N 段」或「生成 N 张」，就照节点此刻的样子发出去。（#1336）
- **不再显示金额合计**：扣多少钱由你的服务商说了算。确认卡和任务中心只写要生成几张、几段，不印合计。（#1339、#1345）
- **只发用得上的参考图**：生成框参考行里淡显、写着「未用到」的图，不会发给模型；要用就点一下那张图。（#1269、#1279）
- **取消就是取消**：确认卡上点「取消」，还没交出去的不会再交；拆解中点「取消拆解」，不会再发新的调用。已经发出去的那几次照常结算。（#1163、#1353）
- **取不回时不再叫你重新生成**：已经交给服务商的任务暂时取不回，节点上写「去重新取回」。它只会再查一次、再下载一次，不会重新生成。（#1290）

## 更快

以下数字都是在一台 Windows 电脑上量的。

- **320 镜的分镜方案约半秒打开**：从点开到能编辑，约 14.6 秒 → 约 0.5 秒。（#1210）
- **看过分镜再切文稿不再卡**：约 7.4 秒 → 不到 0.1 秒。（#1193）
- **拖动大画布更跟手**：全选 180 个节点拖动，每秒 18 帧 → 45 帧；拖一个装着 60 张卡的组框，最卡的那些帧从 53 毫秒降到 15 毫秒。（#1041、#1275）
- **大项目第一张图出来得更早**：300 张图的项目，点开到第一张图约 3.8 秒 → 约 2.9 秒。（#1044）
- **点卡弹生成框不再卡**：画布内容很多时也一样。（#1231）

## 安装与更新

- **有新版本时，顶栏右边出现「新版本 x.y.z」**：点它看这一版改了什么、下载更新。（#1135）
- **自动检查更新**：启动 30 秒后查一次，之后每 6 小时查一次；断网、查不到都不打扰你。（#988）
- **Windows 退出时自动装好**：点「下载更新」后在后台下载，下好后退出 Nomi 时自动安装，也可以点「重启以更新」马上装。（#1135）

## 升级须知

以前在哪 → 现在在哪。都不需要你动手迁移，打开项目就是新样子。

- **「创作内容」列**：变成左栏「文稿」抽屉；创作页工具条最左的「文稿名 ▾」也能切换文稿和分镜方案。
- **上手清单**：从顶栏挪到「设置 › 通用」最上面。
- **节点上的「N 版」胶囊和版本小窗**：换成图片右上角的数字角标，点开就地铺开。
- **成功镜头的「重拍这镜」**：去掉了，点节点上的 ↑ 就是再出一版。
- **「抽帧 ▾」**：改名「截帧 ▾」。
- **连线中间的用途标签**：去掉了。断开连线：悬停或选中连线，点中点的「×」，或者按 Delete。用途写在目标节点参考缩略图的左下角。
- **画布底部的批量生成栏、「生成选中 N 个」、⌘Enter 批量生成**：去掉了。批量出图改用编组：选中编组框，点「生成整组」。
- **「每次生成几个」**：去掉了；模型支持时，用生成框里的「数量」。
- **生成框的「生成方式」页签**：视频的挪进参数面板；图片不再需要，放了参考图就自动用参考。
- **Agent 权限三档**：改成两档。升级后一律是「请求批准」；原来用「全自动」的，要自己重新选「直接生成」，并确认一次。
- **确认卡的翻页、卡上改提示词和换模型**：点卡上的小卡，到画布节点上改。
- **拆解表的「生成 N 镜」按钮**：去掉了；勾选镜头后，告诉 Agent 要做什么。
- **画布上自动出现的「分镜表」节点**：升级后第一次打开项目时移除，镜头节点和分镜方案都在；要按镜序看，切到「列表」。
- **旧导演台和 x-bot 人偶**：换成 3D 导演台和新人偶，旧工程自动迁移。跪、坐地这类动作会换成相近的动作。
- **「群众」**：并进「加人」。
- **编组框的旧颜色**：恢复成默认灰，可以在组工具条的「颜色」里重新选。
- **Skill 库顶上的「全部 / 技能 / 提示词 / 效果」筛选**：去掉了；「效果」在提示词库里。

## 已知问题

- **0.23 及更早的版本不会自己提示这次更新**：请手动「设置 › 关于 › 检查更新」，或到官网下载。
- **剪长视频要等一会**：剪辑是逐帧精确剪、重新编码，卡上显示「剪辑中 · N%」，可以取消。
- **大画板点「完成」要等几秒才存好**：按钮会显示「保存中…」。
- **撤销记录只在这次打开项目期间有效**：关掉项目再打开，之前的操作就撤不回了。
- **「直接生成」档的刹车还有漏洞**：同一轮里新起的草稿拦不住。用这一档时留意对话里的卡，需要时点停止。
- **点「生成」后马上改刚落到画布上的节点，发出去的是改过的**：等卡上出现「正在发出」再改。
- **自配的纯文本模型也会收到附图**：可能报错，或者模型说看不到图。要问图片相关的问题，换一个能读图的模型。
- **网络盘、外接盘上的项目没法在 Nomi 里删除**：那里没有回收站，Nomi 会说明原因并保留项目。
- **改成直连后，界面上没有「恢复到改之前」**：备份在各助手配置文件旁边，文件名后面加了 `.nomi-backup-premigrate`，关掉那个助手后可以手动换回。
- **窗口很窄时，分镜方案每一行的底栏放不下**：英文界面更明显。拉宽窗口即可。
- 模型能不能用，仍然取决于你自己的服务商账号、权限和额度。

## What changed

A new layout, and you can trim video, edit images and browse versions right on the canvas.

**Upgrading from 0.23**: 0.23 and earlier do not check for updates on their own. Open Settings › About and click Check for updates, or download the installer from the website. From 0.24 on, Nomi checks for updates by itself.

### New layout

![Canvas page: ① top bar ② left rail ③ add-node bar ④ Agent bubble](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aqm857886159/Nomi/v0.24.0/docs/release-notes/v0.24.0/en/canvas-shell.png)

- **One top bar**: The project menu on the left (recent projects, back to library, new project, rename), Create / Generate / Preview in the middle, and Tasks, the browser and Settings on the right.
- **A narrow left rail**: Docs, Catalog, Assets and Flows belong to this project; Skills and Prompts are shared by all projects. Each opens as a drawer over the canvas; click again to close it.
- **Three ways to place the Agent**: A bubble, a floating window, or docked on the right. Switch from the panel header; each page remembers its own choice. Ctrl+J (⌘J on Mac) opens it and puts the cursor in the input.
- **Add-node bar at the bottom center**: Image, Video, Audio, Text and Clip in one row, then Import and +. Reset view, minimap and tidy-up moved into the ⋯ menu at the bottom left.
- **Quieter look**: Selected nodes get a thin dark outline instead of a blue frame, and icons come in four consistent sizes.

### Canvas nodes do more

![A node with several versions, spread out in place](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aqm857886159/Nomi/v0.24.0/docs/release-notes/v0.24.0/en/node-versions.png)

- **Browse versions in place**: A node with two or more versions shows a number badge. Click it to spread the versions beside the node, then set one as the main image, download it or delete it. Setting the main image can be undone.
- **Trim video on the canvas**: Click Trim on a video node, drag both ends to keep a section, then click Trim clip to get a new, connected video card. Trimming runs on your computer and does not call a generation model.
- **Capture frames**: Take the first or last frame from Capture frame, or the current frame with the camera button on the video card. Each frame becomes a connected image card.
- **Whiteboards open in place**: No more full-screen window. The whiteboard opens on the canvas with the brush ready, and the drawing shows on the node.
- **Text nodes**: Expand into a prompt, describe an image, translate, split or rewrite. Connect one to an image or video node and it shows up there as Connected text, appended to the prompt. The Agent can read and edit it.
- **One-click variations that wait for you**: Multi-angle grid, next moment, previous moment, three views and four-panel story build a connected node with the prompt filled in. Nothing runs until you click ↑.
- **Undo only undoes your edits**: Ctrl/⌘+Z no longer takes back images or text that already came back from generation.
- **Better groups**: A selected group shows a toolbar with color, arrange, generate the whole group and add to timeline. Cards move with the group when you drag it.

### Agent and spending

![The Agent docked on the right: ① switch placement ② permission level](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aqm857886159/Nomi/v0.24.0/docs/release-notes/v0.24.0/en/agent-docked.png)

- **Two permission levels**: Ask first (default) does undoable work directly and stops before any generation. Auto-run starts generations without asking each time, and stops when the same shot keeps failing or a generation falls outside the plan. Switching to it asks you once.
- **Confirmation cards point at canvas nodes**: The card shows the shots as small cards plus one summary line. Click a small card to edit that node on the canvas; click Generate to send the nodes as they are.
- **No more totals**: Your provider decides what you are charged, so cards and the task center show how many images or videos, not a total amount.
- **Only references in use are sent**: References shown faded as not in use are never sent to the model.
- **Cancel means cancel**: Nothing that has not been handed over is sent after you cancel, and canceling a breakdown starts no new calls. Calls already sent are still billed.
- **No "generate again" when a result can't be fetched**: A submitted job that cannot be fetched yet offers to fetch again, which only checks and downloads. It never generates a new one.

### Faster

Measured on one Windows computer.

- **A 320-shot storyboard opens in about half a second**: From click to editable, about 14.6 s → about 0.5 s.
- **Switching docs no longer stalls after the storyboard**: About 7.4 s → under 0.1 s.
- **Dragging large canvases is smoother**: Dragging 180 selected nodes went from 18 to 45 frames per second; the slowest frames when dragging a 60-card group went from 53 ms to 15 ms.
- **First image in a big project**: In a 300-image project, about 3.8 s → about 2.9 s.

### Settings, install and connections

- **Test connection no longer sends a real generation request**: It reads the model list once.
- **Custom relays use the model name you configured**.
- **Keys saved offline say saved, not verified**: They are checked again when you are online.
- **DeepSeek V3.2 on APIMart is retired**: Every call to it failed.
- **A new-version pill in the top bar**: Click it to read what changed and update.
- **Automatic update checks**: 30 seconds after launch, then every 6 hours.
- **Windows installs the update when you quit**: Or restart to update right away.
- **New Mac installer window**: If macOS blocks the first launch, use Open Anyway in System Settings › Privacy & Security.
- **Export stops when a clip's media info can't be read**: It names the file instead of exporting a silent film.
- **A faster direct connection for external AI tools**: Settings offers to move Claude Code and Codex over, with a backup first. After that, open Nomi before using them.
- **The new UI smoothness stat asks again**: Nothing about it is sent until you agree.

### Where things moved

- **Creation contents column**: Now the Docs drawer in the left rail, or the doc menu at the left of the writing toolbar.
- **Version pill and versions window**: Replaced by the number badge on the image.
- **Retake this shot**: Removed; click ↑ on the node to make another version.
- **Batch generate bar and ⌘Enter**: Removed; select a group and generate the whole group.
- **Generations per click**: Removed; use Quantity when the model supports it.
- **Three Agent permission levels**: Now two. Everyone starts on Ask first after upgrading; choose Auto-run again if you used full auto.
- **Auto-created storyboard table nodes**: Removed the first time you open the project; shots and storyboards stay.
- **Old director stage and x-bot mannequin**: Replaced and migrated automatically; kneeling and sitting poses map to the closest actions.

### Known issues

- **0.23 and earlier will not offer this update by themselves**: Use Settings › About › Check for updates, or download from the website.
- **Trimming long videos takes a while**: Progress shows on the card and you can cancel.
- **Undo history lasts until you close the project**.
- **Auto-run can miss a new draft started in the same turn**: Keep an eye on the cards and press stop if needed.
- **Editing a node right after clicking Generate sends the edited node**: Wait until the card says it is sending.
- **Text-only custom models also receive attached images**: Use a vision model for questions about images.
- **Projects on network or external drives can't be deleted from Nomi**: There is no Trash there; Nomi keeps the project and says why.
- Whether a model works still depends on your own provider account, permissions and quota.
